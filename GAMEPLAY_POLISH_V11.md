# Nightfall Ops v11 — Gameplay responsiveness and presentation

- Increased stride animation cadence and amplitude while keeping idle motion nearly still.
- Added short hit flinch, tint flash, and knockback feedback when a projectile hits a surviving enemy.
- Preserved the location-specific palette/weather/scenery system and per-weapon projectile, recoil, muzzle flash, shell ejection, and audio-slot behavior.
- The Settings menu already exposes master/music/SFX volume, sound enable, aim assist, auto-shoot and range, camera shake, reduced motion, FPS counter, and control scale/position. Those controls remain available and persist through the existing save flow.
